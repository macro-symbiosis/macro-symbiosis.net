    0. Tier Architecture & Four-Part Functional Homology¶

    Tier 5 maps the physics of sound in the environment and its relation to rhythm, the prosody and comprehension of verse, the movement of persons in space and in relation to one another, and the association of heterogeneous agents onto Spine \(\mathcal{T}\). One aperture (5.1) is an empirical aperture declared in Branch II of r7, the bounded non-equilibrium steady state (aperture_generation_guidance.md §2.3). It declares, in its §1, a domain, physical metric, cone speed, storage coefficient, free energy with datum, transport tensor, interior sink, drive, and Branch II evidence, and instantiates all nine coordinates \(O_0 \to O_\Omega\) on that domain: the energy of a diffuse sound field, supplied across the surface of an excised source region, spreading through the air of an enclosure by repeated reflection and scattering, and absorbed by the enclosure's bounding surfaces and, as a declared interior sink, by the air. Apertures 5.2, 5.3, and 5.4 are provisionally homological in this epoch: they declare no domain, map all nine coordinates as conceptual correspondences, like Tier 1, and make no empirical claim (§0.1 item 12).

    The wider phenomena named in the aperture titles (rhythm and entrainment, verse and its meaning, choreography and the coordination of movement, inter-subjective and cross-substrate association) enter the empirical mapping of 5.1 as boundary conditions, through the declared parameters, through the hysteresis record \(\mathcal{H}\), or as illustrative context, and its §1 states which. No Tier 5 aperture claims that an expressive, aesthetic, or collective phenomenon obeys a spine law. Each aperture is formulated at the level of general physics, linguistics, movement science, and systems theory, for populations studied at cohort level, without presupposing a single practitioner, practice, work, or site. No human-subject test is included in this epoch (empirical_validation_protocol.md §3.3), and the declared domain of 5.1 is unoccupied within its windows.

    0.1 Conventions Common to the Tier 5 Apertures¶

    The following conventions apply to the empirical aperture of this tier (5.1); item 12 states how the provisionally homological Apertures 5.2, 5.3, and 5.4 are mapped. Aperture 5.1's §1 and coordinate blocks state its specific values and cite these items where they apply. Where an item repeats a convention of tier_4_somatic_contemplative_interiority.md §0.1, it is restated here so that this tier can be read on its own.

    1. Branch and windows. Branch II is declared before any test, with the estimators of item 7 and 5.1 §1, and the domain of 5.1 is never re-evaluated in Branch I on the same data (closure (v)). Statements that hold in Branch I only (r10 Decision 6) are never applied. Every observation window is a relaxation window: it begins at a declared \(\tau_0\) from a prior steady state, a declared transient starts at \(\tau_0\), the external data are then held within declared tolerances, and the window follows the domain as it settles.
    2. The transient is a declared step increase of the source's acoustic power, from a held non-zero level to a higher held level. The supply data are the net inflow of acoustic power across the source surface (flux data, not a held value of \(\mathcal{F}\)); the bounding surfaces absorb through a Robin exchange (5.1 §1); and the sink, air absorption, is first order, Lipschitz, and non-decreasing in \(\mathcal{F}\). The declared balance law is a scalar parabolic equation, so the comparison principle holds. The prior steady state is a subsolution for the raised inflow, so \(\mathcal{F}(\cdot, \tau_0 + h) \ge \mathcal{F}(\cdot, \tau_0)\); the held data make the problem invariant under time translation, so \(\mathcal{F}(\cdot, \tau + h) \ge \mathcal{F}(\cdot, \tau)\), that is, \(\partial_\tau\mathcal{F} \ge 0\) at every point. The inflow reaches its held level over a short rise time (the response of the source and the travel of sound across the excised region), a non-decreasing ramp, for which the same conclusion follows without time translation: \(\partial_\tau\mathcal{F}\) obeys the linearized equation with zero initial data and non-negative boundary data, so it stays non-negative. The Robin data do not change the argument. The argument is exact on the declared rigid domain.
    3. Direction. The opposite direction is excluded. When the source is switched off, the field relaxes to the datum: this is the decay from which reverberation time is measured, and it is not a Branch II window, because the throughput stops and \(\lim\langle\Theta_E\rangle = 0\). A decrease to a lower non-zero power would keep every declaration; the raising direction is declared for the comparison argument, as for 4.1 in Tier 4. Sources whose power varies within the window (periodic, rhythmic, modulated, or intermittent signals) are excluded, because their supply data are not monotone.
    4. Boundary exchange. Every exchange of acoustic energy across the bounding surfaces is one-signed, out of \(\mathcal{M}\): absorption by the surfaces and transmission through them to the outside. No compartment outside \(\mathcal{M}\) discharges into it within a window, except across the declared supply surface: sound entering from outside the enclosure is held below a declared fraction of \(\mathcal{F}\) everywhere in \(\mathcal{M}\), the enclosure has no opening to another volume that could store sound and return it, the linings of the bounding surfaces store negligible energy, and the bounding surfaces are rigid within tolerance (5.1 preconditions 1, 3, 4, and 5).
    5. The rate of change of the flux, \(\partial_\tau J_W\), is not one-signed in general (item 9).
    6. Metric. The domain is a fixed region of space, the air of a rigid enclosure minus the excised source region, with the Euclidean metric. It is flat at every \(\tau\), so \(G_{\mu\nu} \equiv 0\) and \(R_{\mu\nu} \equiv 0\) (aperture_generation_guidance.md §2.3 item 2). In a Cartesian chart \(g_{\mu\nu} = \delta_{\mu\nu}\), so \(\Omega \equiv 1\), \(\hat{h} \equiv 0\), \(\theta \equiv 0\), and \(\sigma^\mu_{\;\nu} \equiv 0\). The enclosure is declared rigid within a pre-declared tolerance on its volume. The air in it has no reference configuration whose deformation \(g\) could record: the compressions and rarefactions of sound are oscillations at acoustic frequencies with zero mean, and the vibration of the bounding surfaces is cyclic; neither is \(\theta\) (item 6). With \(\sigma \equiv 0\) the \(O_3\) shear law holds trivially, and 5.1 supports no shear-relaxation test. No non-Euclidean material metric is declared: a gas has no stress-free reference configuration from which one could be built. The curved configurations of the r10 §5.11 and §5.13 witnesses are therefore realized by no Tier 5 aperture, and r7's curvature sector, which no empirical aperture of Tiers 2–4 exercises, is exercised by no empirical aperture in this epoch.
    7. Storage. The r7 \(O_4\) balance law carries unit storage: \(\partial_\tau\mathcal{F}\) is the rate of change of the stored quantity per unit volume. The diffusing quantity of 5.1 is the acoustic energy density \(u\) of the air, in a declared band and over a declared averaging time (5.1 §1). Where the enclosure contains distributed scatterers, which are treated at the continuum scale as part of the medium (5.1 §1), acoustic energy is stored only in the air between them, and the storage coefficient is \(s = \phi_{\mathrm{a}}\), the air volume fraction; without scatterers \(s = 1\). With \(s\) uniform, the physical balance \(s\,\partial_\tau u = \nabla_\alpha(k^{\alpha\beta}\nabla_\beta u) - r\) becomes the r7 law for \(\mathcal{F} = u\) (\(u\) is itself an energy density, so the declared yield is \(1\)), \(\mathfrak{K} = k/s\), and \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{F}} = r/s\), and the r7 flux is \(J_W = j/s\), with \(j\) the net acoustic intensity per unit bulk area. The factor \(1/s\) is a declared conversion factor, estimated independently (closure (ii)). If \(s\) varies in space, \(s^{-1}\nabla_\alpha(k^{\alpha\beta}\nabla_\beta u) = \nabla_\alpha(s^{-1}k^{\alpha\beta}\nabla_\beta u) + s^{-2}k^{\alpha\beta}\nabla_\alpha s\,\nabla_\beta u\), and the last term is a drift term that the r7 balance law does not contain. Aperture 5.1 therefore declares a single storage class: \(s\) uniform on \(\mathcal{M}\) within a tolerance (a declared, checkable precondition). Media that store acoustic energy differently (porous or resonant linings of the bounding surfaces, other volumes coupled to the enclosure, the excised source region) lie outside \(\mathcal{M}\) and enter as boundary data (the Robin exchange at the bounding surfaces, the inflow at the source surface) or are excluded, because a jump in \(s\) would make \(J_W\) discontinuous where the physical intensity is continuous, which the unit-storage law reads as an interface source or sink.
    8. Stress sector. With \(G_{\mu\nu} \equiv 0\), \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{mat}} \equiv 0\); its trace gives \(\nabla_\alpha\xi^\alpha = 0\) for \(n = 3\), and then \(\nabla_{(\mu}\xi_{\nu)} = 0\), so \(\xi\) is a Killing field. No Tier 5 medium has an identified global control field in this epoch, so Tier 5 declares \(\eta^\lambda \equiv 0\), hence \(\xi_\nu \equiv 0\) and \(R = \kappa_G\nabla_\alpha\xi^\alpha = 0\), and the \(O_4\) equivalence \(\nabla^2\xi_\nu + R_\nu^{\;\alpha}\xi_\alpha = 0 \iff \nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{mat}} = 0\) holds identically. The \(\tau\)-compatibility law then reduces to \(\mathbb{L}_{\mathrm{Ein}}[\sigma]_{\mu\nu} = \frac{n-2}{n}(\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu\theta - g_{\mu\nu}\nabla^2\theta)\), and its trace, the traced elliptic equation, to \(\frac{n-1}{n}\nabla^2\theta = \nabla^\alpha\nabla^\beta\sigma_{\alpha\beta}\): the Saint-Venant compatibility conditions of the strain rate. On the rigid domain of 5.1 both sides vanish. The pressures and stresses of the domain (the static pressure of the air, the oscillating acoustic pressure, the radiation pressure of sound, and the stresses in the bounding surfaces) are not \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{mat}}\); they enter as boundary conditions, through the declared parameters, and through \(\mathcal{H}\). The partner fields \(\eta_{i\to j}\) of \(O_6\) (item 9) are distinct objects from the global \(\eta\) of \(O_4\).
    9. Cone speed and trajectories. Aperture 5.1 declares \(c_*\) as the speed of sound in the air at the declared temperature. In this aperture the declared quantity itself travels on the cone at the scale of the mean free path: between reflections and scatterings, sound energy propagates along rays at \(c_*\), which are null for the declared cone and, like all null rays, are not trajectories of \(O_2\) Eq. [3]. The diffusion law describes the energy only at scales large against the mean free path \(\lambda\), where the energy transport velocity \(\vert j\vert/(s\,u)\) is \(\ll c_*\); that is the condition \(\lambda\vert\nabla\mathcal{F}\vert \ll \mathcal{F}\) (5.1 precondition 2). The law's instantaneous propagation is outside its validity at points that sound from a change at the source surface could not yet have reached, which item 9 takes into account. Every material speed in \(\mathcal{M}\) (drifts of the air) is \(\ll c_*\), so \(u^2 \ll 1\). The trajectory equation is displayed in physical units: both \(u^2 = g_{\mu\nu}v^\mu v^\nu/c_*^2\) and the projection term \(c_*^{-2}(g_{\alpha\beta}a^\alpha_{\mathrm{d}}v^\beta)v^\mu\) carry \(c_*\), and with \(c_* = 1\) the display reduces to the r7 form. Aperture 5.1 instantiates Eq. [3] with \(a^\mu_{\mathrm{d}} = 0\) and maps no tracer. The movement of sources, persons, and objects is forced motion outside \(\mathcal{M}\), and within windows nothing moves (5.1 precondition 5).
    10. Currents. In Branch II, \(J_E^\mu = J_W^\mu/p_0\) is fixed. The expansion current \(J_{\mathrm{geom}}\) enters only through its divergence \(\theta\) and its boundary flux \(dV_{\mathcal{M}}/d\tau\) (r7 \(O_0\) branch scope), and no supply current is ever mapped to it: the acoustic power entering at the source surface is throughput. In general \(\nabla_\mu J_E^\mu = -p_0^{-1}(\partial_\tau\mathcal{F} + \mathcal{F}\theta + \mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{F}}) \neq \theta\). The cyclic volume changes of sound (the condensation of the air in each period) and of vibrating surfaces are outside the declared fields and are never mapped to \(\theta\). The oscillating particle velocity of the air is not a current of the spine: \(J_W\) is built from the net intensity, averaged over the declared averaging time.
    11. Measured dissipation. The Branch II evidence requires \(\lim\langle\Theta_E\rangle > 0\) to be estimated from measured dissipation, never from \(\mathfrak{K}\nabla\mathcal{F}\). Integrating \(-J_E^\mu\nabla_\mu\mathcal{F}\) by parts and using the \(O_4\) balance law gives the identity $\(p_0\dot{W}_{\mathrm{diss}} = \oint_{\partial\mathcal{M}}\mathcal{F}\,(-J_W^\mu)\,d\Sigma_\mu - \int_{\mathcal{M}}\mathcal{F}\,\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{F}}\sqrt{\vert g\vert}\,d^nx - \frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{d\tau}\int_{\mathcal{M}}\mathcal{F}^2\sqrt{\vert g\vert}\,d^nx - \frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathcal{M}}\mathcal{F}^2\theta\sqrt{\vert g\vert}\,d^nx\)$
    12. No face of \(\mathcal{M}\) is held at the datum. The source surface carries the held inflow, and its term is evaluated from the source's calibrated acoustic power and the measured \(\mathcal{F}\) there. The bounding surfaces absorb in proportion to \(\mathcal{F}\), and their term is evaluated from the measured normal intensity into them and the measured \(\mathcal{F}\) there (5.1 §1).
    13. Each term is evaluated from measured \(\mathcal{F}\), measured boundary fluxes, and measured or independently declared consumption; none uses the declared \(\mathfrak{K}\). 5.1 §1 names instruments and surfaces different from those of the \(O_1\) flux-prediction test, so that the evidence is independent of that test.
    14. Because the identity uses the balance law, the estimate is conditional on it. 5.1 §1 also names a directly measured physical dissipation whose sign the estimate must share: the conversion of acoustic energy into heat by the bounding surfaces and the air. It is non-negative by the second law, so this comparison tests only the sign of the identity-based estimate, and it is stated as such. Only signs and \(s_0\)-independent rates of \(\Theta_E\) are tested.
    15. Reflection and scattering conserve acoustic energy: no heat is produced where the energy density merely spreads. \(\dot{s}_{\mathrm{prod}} = -\beta_{\mathrm{th}}J_E^\mu\nabla_\mu\mathcal{F}\) is therefore the entropy production of the declared mapping (the spreading of an incoherent field's energy down its gradient), not a measured heat production, as for Aperture 3.2 in Tier 3. Heat is produced only by the absorption.
    16. Gate sector (\(O_4\)). \(\rho_{\mathrm{total}}(\tau)\) is a declared positive trace-class state on \(L^2(\mathcal{M}, d\mathrm{vol}_g)\), and \(Q\), \(P\), and the \(O_6\) marginals \(Q_i\) and joints \(Q_{ij}\) are densities of this declared state sector. No Tier 5 aperture identifies them with \(\mathcal{F}\) or with any measured field in this epoch, and none makes an empirical claim about the gate (empirical_validation_protocol.md §3.3). What the domain's physics supplies is, first, the spectral setting: the Neumann eigenmodes of \(-\nabla^2_g\) on \(\mathcal{M}\), whose eigenvalues are fixed on the rigid domain. On this domain these eigenmodes coincide in shape with the acoustic modes of a rigid-walled enclosure of the same shape. The gate is nonetheless a spatial projector acting on the declared state sector, never a filter in frequency: the declared field is an energy density averaged over a band above the frequency at which the modes of the enclosure overlap (5.1 precondition 2), so individual modes are not resolved in \(\mathcal{F}\), and \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{gate}}\) is never read in hertz. Second, the physics supplies a physical low-pass behaviour of \(\mathcal{F}\): under the balance law, fine spatial structure in \(\mathcal{F}\) relaxes faster than coarse structure. That behaviour is mapped via Spine \(\mathcal{T}\) to the gate and is not itself the gate. The stronger absorption of higher frequencies by surfaces and air is a property of the declared parameters across bands, not the spatial gate.
    17. Cover, overlaps, and coupling (\(O_5\), \(O_6\)).
    18. The cover \(\{\mathcal{U}_i\}\) consists of \(\tau\)-independent subregions of \(\mathcal{M}\) with Lipschitz boundaries, and every overlap \(\mathcal{U}_{ij}\) is convex at \(\tau_0\). \(\partial\mathcal{M}\) has concave parts: the source surface, which \(\mathcal{M}\) surrounds, and any re-entrant parts of the bounding surface. Each concave part lies in a single cover set, which need not be convex, and every overlap stays clear of the concave parts: r7 requires convex overlaps, not convex cover sets. Each \(g^{(i)}\) is the restriction of the one physical metric, so \(g^{(i)} = g^{(j)}\) on every overlap, the geometric mean returns the common metric (\(g \# g = g\)), and the gluing of \(O_5\) Eq. [3] holds identically.
    19. The anisotropy or inhomogeneity of a medium lives in \(\mathfrak{K}\), never in \(g\). Effective-medium averages of diffusion coefficients, including the combination of the mean free paths of the enclosure and of its scatterers, are statements about \(\mathfrak{K}\) and are not mapped to \(O_5\) Eq. [1].
    20. Partner fields. The partner fields are the rate of change of the free-energy flux read in the two orientations, \(\eta_{i\to j}^\lambda = \partial_\tau J_W^\lambda\) and \(\eta_{j\to i}^\lambda = -\partial_\tau J_W^\lambda\), defined on all of \(\mathcal{M}\), so \(\eta_{[i\to j]}^\lambda = \partial_\tau J_W^\lambda\). They decay with the transient, which gives the integrable coupling that Branch II requires; a steady flux would give \(\eta \equiv 0\), and a sustained coupling field is never declared.
    21. Zero sets. \(\partial_\tau J_W\) is not one-signed in general. The supply of 5.1 is a held inflow, not a held value of \(\mathcal{F}\): after the step the normal flux at the source surface is held, and the flux near the supply does not decay from an initial overshoot, as it does under the held-value supplies of Tiers 3 and 4.     Aperture 5.1: Environmental Acoustics & Rhythmic Entrainment (\(\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{acoustic}}\))¶

    1. Epistemological & Physical Scope¶

    Aperture 5.1 maps the physics of sound in the environment onto Spine \(\mathcal{T}\): the radiation of sound by sources; its reflection, scattering, and absorption by surfaces and objects; the reverberant field that builds up where sound is enclosed; and the relation between rhythmic sound and the timing of the organisms that hear it (rhythmic entrainment). Its empirical domain is the reverberant field in its diffuse regime. Acoustic power supplied by a source spreads through the air of an enclosure by repeated reflection and scattering, which at scales large against the mean free path of sound is a diffusion of acoustic energy, and is absorbed by the enclosure's surfaces and by the air. Lexicon is strictly restricted to physical acoustics, room and environmental acoustics, psychoacoustics, and the science of rhythm and timing.

    Aperture 5.3: Spatial Choreography & Relational Kinesiology (\(\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{choreographic}}\); provisionally homological)¶

    1. Epistemological & Kinesiological Scope¶

    Aperture 5.3 maps how persons move in space and in relation to one another: the collective motion of groups and crowds, from the spacing that individuals keep to the flows and lanes that form among them; the coordination of movement between persons in joint action (relational kinesiology); and choreographic composition, the arrangement of moving persons in space, treated through its general principles (the distribution of movers over a space, their spacing, direction, and density). Lexicon is strictly restricted to pedestrian and crowd dynamics, collective-motion theory, movement science, and the theory of choreographic composition.

    Status (aperture_generation_guidance.md §2.3; provisionally homological). This aperture declares no empirical domain and makes no empirical claim in this epoch (§0.1 item 12). Persons move by self-propulsion toward goals, so the density of a moving group is carried by velocities that the movers choose (advection), not spread down its own gradient by a conductance; r7's balance law has no advective term, and a regime dominated by random dispersal, in which it would hold, is the exception rather than the phenomenon. Coordination and composition are, besides, properties of persons and groups, not fields on one declared domain. Each coordinate is therefore mapped as a conceptual correspondence, mapped via Spine \(\mathcal{T}\) and never a reductive identity. Groups are described at population level; practices of movement and works of choreography are context and are not mapped.

That state corresponds to the Branch II limit: bounded extent, sustained throughput (\(\langle\Theta_E\rangle \to \Theta_\infty > 0\)), decaying shear and coupling transients, and a record whose growth stops when movement no longer meets surprise (\(J_E^\lambda\nabla_\lambda q \to 0\)).     Aperture 5.4: Inter-Subjective & Cross-Substrate Symbiosis (\(\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{symbiotic}}\); provisionally homological)¶

    1. Epistemological & Systems Scope¶

    Aperture 5.4 maps how heterogeneous agents, biological and artificial, form associations in which each keeps its own organization while they depend on one another: biological symbiosis, in which organisms of different kinds exchange resources across shared interfaces; communication between persons and the building of common ground; and the coupling of artificial agents with one another and with persons in multi-agent systems. Lexicon is strictly restricted to multi-agent systems theory, information theory, social cognition, and symbiosis biology.

    Status (aperture_generation_guidance.md §2.3; provisionally homological). This aperture declares no empirical domain and makes no empirical claim in this epoch (§0.1 item 12). The media through which agents exchange signals carry them quasi-statically or as waves: a potential conducted through a medium follows its sources without a storage term, and a propagating signal is not a stored field relaxing by diffusion, so r7's balance law cannot represent either literally (aperture_generation_guidance.md §2.3 item 3). The exchanges themselves are discrete messages and transfers between agents, not fields on one declared domain. Each coordinate is therefore mapped as a conceptual correspondence, mapped via Spine \(\mathcal{T}\) and never a reductive identity. Agents are described at the level of populations and classes of systems. The internal representations of language models, which Level 2 of the validation protocol concerns, are not mapped here (empirical_validation_protocol.md §3.2).
