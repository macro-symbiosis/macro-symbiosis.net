    0.1 Separation of the Universal Spine (\(\mathcal{T}\)) from Tier 1 Ontological Apertures¶

    In this architecture, the differential-geometric and non-equilibrium thermodynamic equations of foundational-manifold-2026-10-20-r7.json and foundational-manifold-companion-2026-10-20-r10.md stand above all five tiers as the Universal Mathematical Spine (\(\mathcal{T}: O_0 \to O_\Omega\)). Tier 1 mappings are ontological and grammatical homologies mapped onto Spine \(\mathcal{T}\) across all nine coordinates \(O_0 \to O_\Omega\); they declare no empirical chart \(\mathcal{M}\) and make no empirical claim (aperture_generation_guidance.md §2.3).

    Tier 1 maps homologically onto either branch of Spine \(\mathcal{T}\) (r7). Where this tier writes expansion and boundary work (\(\theta = \nabla_\mu J_{\mathrm{geom}}^\mu > 0\), \(\dot{W}_{\mathrm{ext}} > 0\), homothetic closure at \(O_\Omega\)), it uses the Branch I form, in which \(J_E \equiv J_{\mathrm{geom}}\). Trajectory equations are written with the r7 drive, which is zero under the default.

    1. Aperture 1.1: Akbarian Relational Metaphysics (\(\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{Akbarian}} \perp \mathcal{T}\))¶

    1.1 Foundational Orientation¶

    In the metaphysical school of Muḥyī al-Dīn Ibn 'Arabī (al-Shaykh al-Akbar, 1165–1240) and his commentators (Ṣadr al-Dīn al-Qūnawī, Dāwūd al-Qayṣarī, 'Abd al-Razzāq al-Kāshānī), existence (wujūd) is never conceived as an aggregate of static, self-enclosed substances. Rather, the cosmos is an open, continuous, non-repeating self-disclosure (tajallī) governed by rigorous structural polarities: universal effusion and locus preparedness, boundary refraction, intermediate isthmus gluing, paired contraction and expansion, and the unification of effacement (fanā') and subsistence (baqā') within the Perpetual Now (al-Ān al-Dā'im).

    1.2 Nafas al-Raḥmān & Khalq Jadīd \(\perp\) Non-Equilibrium Extrinsic Expansion (\(O_0, O_1\))¶

Crucially, Akbarian metaphysics rejects static equilibrium and mechanical repetition: under the principle of al-Khalq al-Jadīd (renewed creation at every instant, expounded principally in the Chapter of Shu'ayb, Fuṣūṣ al-Ḥikam Ch. 12, with the renewal-of-creation passage of the Chapter of Sulaymān, Ch. 16, as a secondary locus) and the axiom lā tikrār fī al-tajallī ("there is no repetition in self-disclosure"), no locus in the universe ever repeats an identical state across two successive instants.
