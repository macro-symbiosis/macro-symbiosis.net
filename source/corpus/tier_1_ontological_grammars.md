In classical Chinese natural philosophy (*Shangshu Hongfan*, Zou Yan, *Huainanzi*, *Huangdi Neijing*), **Wu Xing** (五行) denotes five kinetic phase-transitions rather than static elements; *Xing* (行) literally signifies **"movement, conduct, or active passage."** *Wu Xing* formalizes open non-equilibrium regulation as a **five-node cyclic control network** forming a regular 10-arc directed tournament that orients the complete undirected graph $K_5$, decomposed into two edge-disjoint Hamiltonian directed cycles:

1. **The *Sheng* (生, Generation / Feed-Forward Excitation) Cycle ($C_5^{(+1)}$, shift $+1 \bmod 5$):**
   $$\mathrm{Water}\;(\text{水}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Wood}\;(\text{木}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Fire}\;(\text{火}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Earth}\;(\text{土}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Metal}\;(\text{金}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Water}\;(\text{水})$$

   $$\mathrm{Water}\;(\text{水}) \dashrightarrow \mathrm{Fire}\;(\text{火}) \dashrightarrow \mathrm{Metal}\;(\text{金}) \dashrightarrow \mathrm{Wood}\;(\text{木}) \dashrightarrow \mathrm{Earth}\;(\text{土}) \dashrightarrow \mathrm{Water}\;(\text{水})$$
