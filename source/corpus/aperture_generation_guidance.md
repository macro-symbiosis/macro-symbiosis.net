## 1. Architectural Separation: The Invariant Spine ($\mathcal{T}$) vs. Evolvable Aperture Basis Sets ($\{\mathcal{E}\}$)

To prevent category confusion and allow the framework to scale across diverse geographical, cultural, and scientific contexts, this methodology enforces a strict separation between two architectural layers:

### 1.1 The Immutable Mathematical Spine ($\mathcal{T}$)
The differential-geometric and non-equilibrium thermodynamic specification locked in `foundational-manifold-2026-10-20-r7.json` and `foundational-manifold-companion-2026-10-20-r10.md` ($O_0 \to O_\Omega$) is **not** one aperture among many. It is the **candidate substrate-independent mediating structure ($\mathcal{T}$)** onto which epistemological, physical, biological, somatic, and expressive apertures are mapped. Whether these mappings constitute a structure-preserving functor latent in the source disciplines, rather than one imposed by the act of mapping, is the hypothesis $H_1$ tested in `empirical_validation_protocol.md` §1.2. Its 39 equation blocks across coordinates $O_0 \to O_\Omega$, index valences, regularity classes, and standing hypotheses remain invariant across all domains.

### 1.2 Evolvable Orthogonal Aperture Basis Sets ($\{\mathcal{E}_{k,m}\}$)
An **Orthogonal Aperture** $\mathcal{E}_{k,m}$ (where $k$ indexes the organizational tier and $m$ indexes the slot within that tier) is a formal specification of a distinct scientific discipline, philosophical lineage, linguistic calculus, or embodied modality mapped homomorphically onto Spine $\mathcal{T}$.

Unlike Spine $\mathcal{T}$, **no specific list or grid of apertures is permanently frozen**. An aperture array is a *working topological basis* chosen to span the degrees of freedom of a given inquiry:
* **Portability:** Any slot may be re-instantiated with another discipline, tradition, or language family that addresses the same structural problem, under the protocol of §2.2 and the level-of-description rule of §3.1, while preserving the exact operator mappings to Spine $\mathcal{T}$.
* **Evolutionary Refinement Across Research Stages:** Even within a single research program, the selection and grouping of apertures is expected to evolve as empirical findings accumulate. Validating the mathematical skeleton $\mathcal{T}$ requires locking a coherent working basis set for each experimental milestone while maintaining a transparent protocol for modifying, splitting, merging, or substituting apertures in future iterations.

### 2.2 Protocol for Modifying the Aperture Basis Set
When substituting apertures or extending the tier system to a new disciplinary focus or evolutionary iteration, synthetic active states should follow three rules:
1. **Preserve Span Across Scales:** Any modified basis set must retain coverage across abiotic physics, living ecology, embodied interiority, and relational/symbolic expression so that Spine $\mathcal{T}$ is exercised across both material and informational substrates.
2. **Document Basis Substitutions Explicitly:** When substituting or adding an aperture, explicitly state which functional slot(s) and tensor coordinates ($O_0 \to O_\Omega$) the new aperture spans.
3. **Follow the Native Topology Inside Each Aperture:** Within any individual aperture, never force the exposition into a mechanical checklist if the domain's native logic is organized around dual feedback cycles, a grammatical paradigm, or a specific boundary layer. Let the discipline's authentic structure lead the prose while mapping its operators rigorously onto $\mathcal{T}$.

* **Branch II** ($H_0 = 0$) is a bounded non-equilibrium steady state, verified by the `r10` §5.13 witness.

The apertures fall into three groups:

* **Tier 1** apertures are ontological and grammatical homologies. They map onto all nine coordinates $O_0 \to O_\Omega$, in either branch, as conceptual correspondences. They declare no empirical domain and make no empirical claim.
* **Twelve empirical apertures in Tiers 2–5** (all except 4.3, 5.2, 5.3, and 5.4) instantiate all nine coordinates on their declared domains and declare **Branch II**.    * $\mathcal{F}$ carries a declared **absolute datum** $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{ref}}$, with $\mathcal{F} \ge \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{ref}}$ throughout $\mathcal{M}$. Every first-order sink is $k(\mathcal{F} - \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{ref}}) \ge 0$.

**Binding constitutive closure (r7 $O_4$).** Every non-default $\mathfrak{K}$, $\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{F}}$, or $a_{\mathrm{d}}$ must satisfy four conditions:

* (i) it is a named law with its functional form fixed before the test;

## 3. The Zero-Leakage Holdout Standard
To preserve the empirical validity of downstream out-of-distribution testing, all Stage I and Stage II theoretical specifications must enforce **strict independence from held-out empirical test contexts** across two levels:

### 3.1 Direct Lexical Independence
Specifications must contain zero proper nouns, geographic coordinates, institutional identifiers, regional landmarks, internal application variable names, or biographical narratives associated with any downstream empirical test site or practitioner. Tier 1 traditions and language families are described only at the level of the family and its comparative reconstruction or of the classical tradition, never through subgroups below the family level, local communities, or localized geography.

### 3.2 Indirect Semantic & Structural-Proxy Independence
Removing proper nouns is necessary but not sufficient. If a theoretical specification illustrates its principles using hyper-specific scenarios that mirror a held-out test set, it introduces **structural-proxy leakage**.
* **The Universal Formulation Rule:** All mechanisms, boundary conditions, and illustrative dynamics must be formulated in **universal, textbook-general terms** derived strictly from the first principles of the discipline under consideration.
* **Prohibition of Cross-Tier Motif Clusters:** Specifications must never repeat a signature cluster of site-specific motifs across multiple tiers, and conditioning documents must never enumerate held-out site features, concretely or by category, where they could function as a positive descriptor of $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{test}}$. The categories auditors use for this check are maintained in a withheld auditor-only annex (`annex_a_auditor_motif_hygiene.md`) and are not reproduced in the conditioning corpus.
* **Population-Level Formulation:** Somatic, contemplative, and expressive dynamics are described at the level of populations or cohorts, with example modalities rotated across apertures, never through a single recurring practitioner profile.
* **During Iteration:** Auditing instances must actively scan for and generalize any phrasing that over-constrains a universal law to a narrow, idiosyncratic scenario that would bias blind empirical evaluation.

## 4. Core Principles of Epistemological Orthogonality ($\perp$)

### 4.1 Orthogonality vs. Reductive Syncretism
In an inner-product or Hilbert space, two non-zero subspaces $\mathcal{E}_A$ and $\mathcal{E}_B$ are orthogonal ($\mathcal{E}_A \perp \mathcal{E}_B$) when their inner product vanishes ($\langle u, v \rangle = 0$ for all $u \in \mathcal{E}_A, \; v \in \mathcal{E}_B$), so that the orthogonal projection of one subspace directly onto the other is identically zero.
* **Non-Reduction of Ontologies:** Never assert a reductive identity between a domain's native concepts and a differential-geometric object (avoiding reductive identity claims between domain concepts and geometric operators). Scientific, linguistic, and contemplative traditions carry irreducible historical, contextual, and phenomenological hysteresis that cannot be collapsed into differential equations; conversely, differential geometry carries strict requirements of dimensional homogeneity, index valence, and falsifiability that cannot be replaced by metaphor.
* **Functorial Correspondence Across Relational Operators:** Correspondence between orthogonal domains $\mathcal{E}_A$ and $\mathcal{E}_B$ is established through a **structure-preserving homomorphism (functor)** mediated by Spine $\mathcal{T}$ ($\mathcal{E}_A \leftrightarrow \mathcal{T} \leftrightarrow \mathcal{E}_B$). Each orthogonal aperture is a candidate formalization of a distinct discipline or tradition, hypothesized under $H_1$ (`empirical_validation_protocol.md` §1.2) to have independently developed operational calculi that map via Spine $\mathcal{T}$ to six structural problems of open, non-equilibrium systems:
  1. **Throughput & Emergent Time ($O_0, O_1$):** Absorbing continuous exogenous flux and sustaining positive entropy production without collapsing into static equilibrium.
  2. **Constraint Stiffness, Volume Insulation & Hysteresis ($O_2, O_3$):** Navigating extrinsic curvature while algebraically isolating the volume element from anisotropic shear and consolidating transduced variance into structural memory.
  3. **Selective Boundary Gating & Stress Conservation ($O_4$):** Filtering high-spatial-wavenumber ambient noise into low-eigenvalue spatial modes while conserving load-bearing boundary stress.
  4. **Non-Annexing Coalescence ($O_5$):** Fusing distinct subsystems across shared overlap zones via the Riemannian geometric mean into a contiguous macroscopic medium without erasing local metrics, supporting lossless circulation around closed spatial cycles.
  5. **Asymmetric Coupling & Mutual Information ($O_6$):** Pairing directional phase-lead asymmetry and trace-free stretching/folding under explicit non-degeneracy and flux-positivity hypotheses across a geodesically convex interface.
  6. **Impedance Relaxation & Homothetic Stationarity ($O_7, O_\Omega$):** Relaxing spatial impedance gradients via a gauge connection and settling of form ($\partial_\tau\tilde{g}_{\mu\nu} \to 0$). In the $\tau \to \infty$ limit ($O_\Omega$), empirical apertures approach the Branch II steady state (bounded volume, sustained throughput), and Tier 1 homologies may map either branch (§2.3).

### 4.2 Strict Lexicon Isolation
Each aperture specification must preserve the integrity of its native technical or traditional vocabulary. Do not blend terminology from unrelated cultural traditions or adjacent tiers within a single aperture (for example, never import terminology from a Tier 1 metaphysical ontology into a Tier 5 poetics or systems aperture, and never import civil-engineering terminology into pure continuum fluid dynamics), and do not re-introduce retired or unverified metaphors (such as calling agents or organisms *"solitons"*) that lack an explicit operator definition in `r7` or `r10`.

## 6. Protocol for Iterative Auditing and Shear Elimination
When a synthetic active state is tasked with reviewing or iterating upon an existing set of aperture specifications, it must execute a structured four-axis diagnostic audit before approving or modifying the text:

1. **Axis I — Mathematical & Indexical Audit:**
   * Verify that every equation, tensor index, contraction, sign convention, standing hypothesis, and regularity class matches `r7` and `r10` exactly (including the traced elliptic $\theta$-equation and complete r7 trajectory equation with its cone-preserving drive in $O_2$, explicit tensor indices on $(\hat{g}_\infty)_{\mu\nu}$ in $O_3$, spatial low-pass projector $\hat{\mathcal{P}}_{\Lambda_{\mathrm{gate}}}$ and global $Q$ vs. marginal $Q_i$ distinction in $O_4$ vs. $O_6$, explicit hypothesis labels on $M^{(ij)} \neq 0$ and $\frac{d\mathcal{I}}{d\tau} > 0$ in $O_6$, strict non-universalization of `r10` §5.10–§5.12 witness constructions and numerical ranges, $O_1$ sign checks against the domain's physical transport direction, and consistency of each aperture's §2.3 declarations at every coordinate).
2. **Axis II — Thermodynamic & Dynamical Audit:**
   * Verify that openness is preserved at every coordinate ($\Theta_E > 0$, $\dot{s}_{\mathrm{prod}} > 0$, $\dot{W}_{\mathrm{tot}} > 0$; $\theta > 0$ and $\dot{W}_{\mathrm{ext}} > 0$ in Branch I only), that each aperture declares its branch before any test (closure (v)) and never relies on a statement of the other branch, that every non-default drive, transport tensor, or sink is declared under the binding closure (named law, finite pre-declared parameters, independent estimation, pre-declared tolerances, default comparison), that no quantity mapped to $\theta$ oscillates in sign, that monotone relaxation is never conflated with periodic oscillation, and that finite-window claims are rate, residual, sign, or flux-prediction tests (§5.5).
3. **Axis III — Epistemological & Lexical Audit:**
   * Verify that the domain's native conceptual architecture is represented accurately according to primary scholarship in that discipline, using functorial correspondence language (`"mapped via Spine \mathcal{T} to"`) without reductive identities or cross-aperture lexical mixing, and that no conditioning document asserts as fact a hypothesis that `empirical_validation_protocol.md` is designed to test.
4. **Axis IV — Holdout & Leakage Audit:**
   * Scan the text for any direct proper-noun leakage, regional linguistic signaling, or indirect structural-proxy motifs that could telegraph a held-out test site (using the categories in the withheld `annex_a_auditor_motif_hygiene.md`), replacing any suspect passages with universally portable formulations.
